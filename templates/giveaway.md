# Giveaway template

Giveaways are the only single posts, and they follow the carousel rules: beautiful first, then the information.

**Front matter:** `format: giveaway`, `template: G1 Giveaway`, plus `market:`, `prize:`, `starts:`, `ends:`.
**Size:** 1080x1350 (4:5). Default is a 4-slide carousel. The quick version is slide 1 alone, with the steps in the caption.

## Structure

| Slide | What it shows | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Cover | The prize looking its best: the four-pack in a setting that fits the market (desert or sea for Oman, the city for the UAE) | Title: GIVEAWAY, plus the prize in one line, e.g. 3 FOUR-PACKS. 3 WINNERS. |
| 2. The product | A colour field or ingredient macro | Label + body: what Zafra is, from `brand/facts.md` (sparkling, 36 mg of saffron, lemon, rose water) |
| 3. How to enter | A clean bottle still with space for text | Label HOW TO ENTER + numbered steps in the body |
| 4. The fine print | A calm closing image | Dates, eligibility, how winners are picked and announced |

A story set runs alongside: 3 to 4 frames pointing to the post (`templates/story.md`, lifestyle set with the post shared on the last frame).

## Before design: required facts
Write them in the draft's front matter. Any `TODO` keeps the piece BLOCKED.
- Start and end dates
- Eligibility: country, minimum age
- Entry steps (e.g. follow @drinkzafra, like, tag a friend)
- How winners are picked and when and where they are announced
- How prizes reach winners
- Any promotion rules Zafra's team follows in that market

## Rules
- Prize described exactly as in `products/` (four-pack of 190 ml bottles).
- No price on a giveaway unless it is in `products/` and under 30 days old.
- Never "free" as a hook line; the prize is the hook.
