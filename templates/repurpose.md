# Repurpose: one source, many pieces

The Bower move. Every source is cropped several ways before we call it used up.

## How
1. Open the source (use the master in `assets/*/masters/` when there is one).
2. Find 3 to 6 held crops: wide, tight, a detail (label, cap, threads, liquid, ice, hand, horizon).
3. Write each crop into `assets/reuse-ideas.md` with its best format.
4. Spread the crops across formats and weeks: never the same crop twice in feed within 8 weeks (check `times_used` and `last_used`).

## One source, five formats (example: zaf-video-007, bottle on sand, the wave)
| Format | Crop | Use |
|---|---|---|
| World reel | Shoreline wide, cream logo | The beach postcard |
| Product reel | Label macro and the cap, 0.6 s each | Two of the fast cuts |
| Carousel | Glow and shadow (still), the wave (video slide) | Story recipe, beach season |
| Story set | Shoreline tight, the wave, label macro, glow | A retailer push in four frames |
| Giveaway | Glow and shadow | Slide 4 background |

Crops and coordinates: `assets/reuse-ideas.md`.
