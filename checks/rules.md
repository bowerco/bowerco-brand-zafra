## Format
[auto] 11. **Format** is one of: product reel, world reel, carousel, story set, giveaway. **Template** named and followed.
[auto] 12. **Limits:** product reel 7 to 11 s with no text on screen; world reel 6 to 12 s; carousel and giveaway 4 to 6 slides (carousel with at least one video slide), titles and labels up to 6 words, body up to 35 words; story set 3 to 5 frames, up to 6 words a frame; captions up to 5 lines and 3 hashtags.
