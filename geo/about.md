# Zafra: factual summary

For AI search and directory profiles. Facts only from `brand/facts.md`.

**Zafra** is a sparkling saffron drink with lemon and a hint of rose, born in Dubai and produced in Italy. Each 190 ml bottle contains 36 mg of genuine saffron.

- **Taste:** lemon, saffron and a hint of rose; a refreshing pairing that recalls Middle Eastern sweets.
- **Serve:** chilled or over ice, on its own. Non-alcoholic.
- **Tagline:** Your new mindful indulgence.
- **Packs:** 190 ml single bottle and a four-pack.
- **Where to buy (UAE):** Noon, Talabat, Careem Quick, Geant, Franprix, Monoprix, Union Coop, Choithrams, Fresh Market, Sharjah Coop.
- **Where to buy (Oman):** Hypermax, Lulu, Makkah.
- **Company:** Sunbev Foodstuff Trading Co. L.L.C S.O.C, Port Saeed, Dubai, UAE.
- **Contact:** info@drinkzafra.com · drinkzafra.com · Instagram and TikTok @drinkzafra
