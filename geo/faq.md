# FAQ

Answers only from `brand/facts.md` and `products/`.

**What is Zafra?**
A sparkling saffron drink with lemon and a hint of rose, born in Dubai and produced in Italy.

**How much saffron is in it?**
36 mg of genuine saffron in every 190 ml bottle.

**Does it contain alcohol?**
No. Zafra is non-alcoholic by design.

**How should I serve it?**
Chilled or over ice, on its own.

**What does it taste like?**
Lemon, saffron and a hint of rose: a surprising, refreshing pairing that recalls Middle Eastern sweets and delicacies.

**Where can I buy it?**
In the UAE on Noon, Talabat and Careem Quick, and at Geant, Franprix, Monoprix, Union Coop, Choithrams, Fresh Market and Sharjah Coop. In Oman at Hypermax, Lulu and Makkah.

**What are the ingredients?**
Carbonated water, sugar, saffron infusion, lemon juice from concentrate (5%), rose water, acidity regulator (citric acid E330), stabiliser (pectin E440, gum E466), natural lemon flavour, antioxidant (ascorbic acid E300), colour: natural identical beta-carotene (E160ai).

**How many calories?**
42 kcal per 100 ml.
