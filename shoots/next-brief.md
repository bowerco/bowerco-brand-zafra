# Next shoot brief

Built from `shoots/gaps.md` (2026-11-24). Two half-days cover everything: a studio half-day and a location half-day.

## Rules for every shot
- **Camera locked off on a tripod.** Never move the camera while recording. The movement comes from the subject.
- Shoot video in 4K (3840x2160 or 2160x3840), 25 fps; 50 or 60 fps for pours and bubbles. Record 10 to 15 s per take.
- Leave room around the subject: we crop into the frame later and hold the crop.
- Label clean, flat and readable whenever the bottle is in frame. Fingerprint-free, natural condensation only.
- No stemmed glassware, no shakers, nothing that implies a cocktail. Zafra on its own, chilled or over ice.
- Seamless paper or painted fields in the brand palette: Zafra Red #F5471C, Soft Cream #FFF9DA, Royal Saffron Violet #94167E, Saffron Glow #FFC13C.
