# Visual

How Zafra looks. Source: Zafra Brand Guidelines v1.2 (November 2025). Exact values live in `brand/rules.yaml` and `brand/tokens.json`; this file says how to use them. Positioning: `brand/story.md`. How each format looks (reels, carousels, stories, giveaways): `templates/`.

## The look in one line

The launched Zafra: bold single-colour fields (coral, red, violet, cream), hard sun, the label big and readable, held frames, cream type. Four references, one per job: **Erewhon** (product reels), **Jacquemus** (world reels), **Rhode** (carousels), **Limousine stories** (story sets). Reference only: never copy a line, layout or visual.

## Brand codes

Repeat these so people recognise Zafra in one or two seconds. **Use at least two in every hero asset** (brand book).
1. **Wordmark:** the zafra script, as a logo file or on the label, cap or patch. The butterfly-petal marque is a companion, never locked to it.
2. **Zafra in glass:** the saffron glow through clear glass.
3. **Warm, sun-led light:** never harsh or clinical.
4. **36 mg saffron:** a signature, used sparingly but consistently as proof of quality.
5. **Origin line:** "Born in Dubai, produced in Italy." intact, close to the pack or product name.
6. **Product presence:** label and bottle shown clearly.
7. **Brand colour palette:** one lead hue per asset.
8. **Materials with quiet luxury:** glass, linen, ceramic, stone.

## Logo

A precise script wordmark, lowercase "zafra". It should read like a signature: clear, never stylised. The **butterfly-petal marque** is a separate companion graphic: it may appear in layouts but must never be locked to the wordmark. (Petal file not supplied yet.)

Files in `assets/logos/`: every version as **SVG** (web, digital, the master), **PDF** and **EPS** (print) and, for most, **PNG** (only where SVG isn't accepted).

| **Primary: cream** | `zafra-logo-cream` | Default for almost everything. Zafra Red, Saffron Glow, other dark or calm grounds. Always this (or yellow) on photos, video and motion. |

**Clear space:** 1x the height of the capital "Z" on all sides. Fallback for billboards or tiny UI badges: 0.5x the Z height, never less.
**Minimum size:** 120 px wide on screen, 18 mm in print.
**Never:** distort, stretch, condense, rotate, outline, add effects, recolour outside the approved versions, place on low-contrast or busy areas, or lock it with other graphics (petal included). Never retype "zafra" in a font.

## Colour

Colour supports recognition and mood; it must feel restrained and intentional.
- **Lead each asset with one primary hue, plus at most one soft accent.**
- Let colour appear through materials (glass, linen, ceramic, stone) more than heavy blocks. Don't introduce new saturated tones.
- **Zafra Red:** brand-signature moments (sign-offs, title slides, corporate pages, shippers, branded headers). Solid background with the cream logo by default.
- **Saffron Glow:** welcoming, sun-warmed fields, and the hero background for **Zero Sugar**. Preferred when a heavy red field isn't needed.
- **Soft Cream:** space and typography backgrounds; the preferred logo colour.
- Zafra Deep Red: accent for depth. Magenta Bloom, Royal Saffron Violet: the floral side of saffron. Petal Mist, Leaf Accent: sparingly.

## Typography

Type should read calm, refined and human: headline for character, text for ease. Keep layouts airy; fewer styles, better chosen.

- **Euclid Flex** (headlines, short leads, buttons, labels). Weights Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold. **ALL CAPS for every headline, title and button.** Short intros may be Euclid Flex at large sizes (20 to 35 pt), 2 to 5 lines max. *Trial files in `assets/fonts/euclid-flex-TRIAL/` (2026-11-20): use them for every draft so type is consistent, but nothing set in them may be published or sent out until the licensed files arrive (Zafra holds the licence).*
- **Acumin Variable Concept** (body: carousel body text, captions on graphics, small text). *Files not supplied yet* (Adobe Fonts). Until they arrive, drafts set body in Euclid Flex Regular, sentence case, and flag it.
- **Arabic pairings:** 29LT Bukra (headlines), **Rubik** (body; files in `assets/fonts/`, free OFL licence). Our content is English only, so Rubik is only for Arabic text.
- One headline size and one text size per layout. Generous spacing; no cramped paragraphs.
- Trial fonts: drafts only. Nothing set in the trial files is posted or sent out until the licensed files replace them.

## Photography and film

Sun-led, cinematic and gracious: an invitation into quiet hospitality. Premium without spectacle; the viewer can almost feel the temperature of the glass.

**Light:** golden-hour character, gentle natural highlights; never cool or clinical. Key from one side (often upper left), large diffused source, soft fill. Keep detail in whites and liquid; no harsh highlights or hard HDR.
**Grade:** warm neutral balance, subtle contrast, clean blacks. Let Saffron Glow read naturally in the glass. No teal-orange, heavy saturation or cyan casts.
**Composition:** calm, spacious, negative space. Hero angles 3/4 front and straight-on; macro for label and liquid. Level horizons; no Dutch angles or busy backgrounds.
**People:** natural, unforced gestures. Linen, silk, cotton in warm muted tones; no graphics, sportswear or logos. Fashion-editorial, impeccable fit. Modest and respectful for GCC contexts: appropriate coverage, no nightclub cues.
**Surfaces:** glass, linen, ceramic, stone, wood. **No barware implying alcohol** (no stemmed coupes, no shakers). No tech objects or plastic gloss. Uncluttered.
**Product:** bottle clean, fingerprint-free, natural minimal condensation. Served on its own, ideally chilled; ice optional and clear. Zero Sugar: Saffron Glow backgrounds, brighter tables.
**Packshots:** bottle front-on, vertical, no tilt; lens at centre height, minimal perspective; calm neutral background, soft natural shadow; label flat and fully readable; consistent scale.
**Copy on images:** primary (cream or yellow) wordmark over a calm, high-contrast area. Never the red logo on photography. Minimal copy, generous margins.
**On-screen text colour (rule, Emi 2026-11-20):** every word on screen (reels, stories, carousel slides, announcements) is **Soft Cream #FFF9DA or white #FFFFFF**. Never red, violet, yellow or any other colour, on photos, video or solid fields.
**Lenses:** people 50 to 85 mm; macro 90 to 105 mm; tables 35 to 50 mm; nothing wider than 28 mm. f/2.8 to f/8.
**Film:** 24/25 fps master; 50/60 fps only for slow-motion pours. Quiet room tone, soft foley (pour, glass, linen), restrained music.
**Still camera while it plays (rule, Emi 2026-11-19):** the frame never moves while a shot is playing. Jacquemus style: all movement comes from what is happening in the shot (a pour, a hand, linen, steam, light, a passing bottle), never from the camera.

- **Never during playback:** zoom in or out, push-ins, pans, tilts, dollies, arcs, handheld drift, and in the edit no animated digital zooms, Ken Burns moves or animated reframes on clips or stills.
- **Encouraged before playback:** zoom into any photo or video and hold that frame. Crop a detail (cap, label, threads, liquid, ice, a hand, the denim patch), set the crop, then let the clip play inside it untouched. One photo or clip can give several different fixed frames. This is how we get the most out of every asset.
- **Cuts do the work:** to change the view, cut to a new held frame (a wider shot, a tighter crop, another clip). A sequence can cut between several crops of the same source.
- **Quality floor:** a crop must still hold up at the output size (1080 px wide for 4:5 and 9:16). If it goes soft, use a wider crop.
