# Pillars

Two layers. New pillars come from Zafra or Emi, never from AI.

## Brand pillars (what Zafra stands for)

From the brand book. Every piece expresses at least one.

| Brand pillar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Heritage, not hype** | Genuine saffron and careful craft, rooted in centuries of ritual. Confident, without loud claims. |
| **Quiet saffron luxury** | Light-kissed warmth, a gentle saffron glow and a refined floral lift. |
| **Modern ritual** | A graceful way to mark the moment: day or night, alone, with friends or family. |

## Content pillars (what we post about)

Set with Zafra on 2026-11-18. Each piece names its slug in front matter (`pillar:`). Shares are a starting point: adjust once `results/` has data.

| # | Pillar | Slug | Job | Main formats | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Quiet Saffron Luxury | `quiet-saffron-luxury` | Make people want it: the product as a beautiful object | Product reel, carousel (product feature) | 30% |
| P2 | Modern Ritual | `modern-ritual` | Build the world and show when to drink it | World reel, lifestyle story set, carousel (story) | 25% |
| P3 | Saffron, Explained | `saffron-explained` | Why saffron, the ingredients, the history | Carousel | 20% |
| P4 | Where to Find Zafra | `where-to-find-us` | Turn interest into purchase | Retailer and where-to-find-us story sets, carousel | 15% |
| P5 | Heritage, Not Hype | `heritage-not-hype` | Earn trust: born in Dubai, produced in Italy, 36 mg of genuine saffron | Carousel, world reel (desert) | 10% |

Giveaways sit under whichever pillar their cover serves (usually P1 or P4).

| Format | Purpose | Pillars |

## What each pillar can say

- **P1 Quiet Saffron Luxury:** the glow, the label, the bubbles, the pour. Shortlines from `brand/voice.md`.
- **P2 Modern Ritual:** hosting, pausing, gifting, seasons (beach season, Ramadan, Eid). Always on its own, chilled or over ice.
- **P3 Saffron, Explained:** only `brand/facts.md`: the red threads of the purple crocus, three per bloom, harvested by hand; soft floral notes, gentle warmth, a naturally golden hue; 36 mg of genuine saffron per bottle; naturally uplifting. Benefit lines beyond that need Zafra's written approval first.
- **P4 Where to Find Zafra:** only stockists in `products/where-to-buy.yaml`, prices only from `products/` and under 30 days old.
- **P5 Heritage, Not Hype:** "Born in Dubai, produced in Italy." kept intact; Gulf hospitality; Italian production (factory footage as supporting frames, never hero).
