## Health claims: read this first

Zafra is a **functional drink, not a medicinal one.** The brand's own instruction: marketing must not make any specific health claims.

Internal background only, **never in copy**:
- The brand describes saffron as a clinically proven natural antidepressant, the strongest found naturally.
- The brand says each bottle carries a clinically proven dose of saffron that creates a mood-lifting effect.

What copy can say (the brand's own wording):
- **"Naturally uplifting."** (approved shortline in the brand book), "calm", "uplifting feeling".
- "Mindful indulgence".
- "36 mg of saffron per bottle": a brand code, used sparingly but consistently as proof of quality, never as a dose.
- Genuine saffron, not just flavouring.

Research line: **pending, not usable yet.** Verbal OK from Zafra relayed by Emi (2026-11-18); written approval and a regulatory OK for UAE and Oman still needed (`_gaps.md`). `./check` blocks it until then:
- "Studies suggest 28 to 30 mg of saffron a day may help ease low mood."

Never: antidepressant, clinically proven, dose, depression, anxiety, treatment, cure, energy boost, performance, or any promise that it fixes, prevents or treats anything. `brand/rules.yaml` (`words.never_use`) blocks these automatically.

"Mood-lifting" appears in the mission but not in the brand book's copy lines: prefer "naturally uplifting" in public copy.
