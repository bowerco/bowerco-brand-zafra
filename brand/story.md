# Story

Where Zafra came from, what it stands for, and who it's for. Facts: `brand/facts.md`. Audience: `brand/audience.md`. Pillars: `brand/pillars.md`.

## What it is

Zafra is a sophisticated saffron drink for modern hospitality: a quiet moment of Middle Eastern warmth, poured with poise. **Born in Dubai, produced in Italy.** Each bottle holds 36 mg of saffron. Lemon, saffron and a hint of rose. It has launched and is sold in the UAE and Oman.

**Tagline:** Your new mindful indulgence

Zafra's own "Our Story" headline and intro lines (A Golden Moment of Hospitality; Born in Dubai, Crafted in Italy; saffron as "red gold"): `brand/voice.md`.

## Why saffron

Saffron is the heart of the drink and the reason it exists: genuine saffron, not flavouring, rooted in centuries of ritual. It gives Zafra its natural golden hue, soft floral aroma and gentle depth, and a naturally uplifting feeling. Functional, never medicinal: the story is about how it feels, never about treating anything (see `brand/facts.md`).

## Mission

Deliver saffron's mood-lifting benefits in a modern, functional beverage, so people can enjoy a delicious drink while feeling its uplifting effects.

## The taste

A surprising, refreshing pairing that recalls Middle Eastern sweets and delicacies. Best served chilled or over ice.

## Brand essence (brand book)

Zafra is a quiet moment of hospitality: Middle Eastern warmth, poured with poise, an ode to Gulf hospitality. Born in Dubai, produced in Italy. It carries the ease of a beautifully hosted table: calm, gracious, assured. Each bottle holds 36 mg of saffron for a natural glow, a soft floral aroma and a gentle depth of flavour. A finish that makes everyday moments a little more golden.

## Positioning

A sophisticated saffron drink for modern hospitality. A luxury non-alcoholic alternative to aperitifs, crafted to be savoured on its own. Serve chilled or over ice.

**We are:** a refined saffron drink for modern hospitality; a high-end botanical drink.
**We are not:** an energy drink, a syrup, a soda, a novelty flavour or a cocktail base.

## Reasons to believe

- **Authentic saffron, not just flavouring.** The glow and soft floral depth of 36 mg of saffron.
- **Italian artistry and standards.** Made in Italy for consistent balance and a harmonious finish.
- **Heritage in every sip.** A gracious welcome from the Gulf, with quiet confidence.
- **Refined sensory signature.** Measured sweetness, soft floral notes, elegant without excess.

## How the brand shows up now (post-launch)

The brand book was written before launch and describes a quiet, golden-hour, hosted-table look. The launched brand is bolder and more playful: a script wordmark on a purple saffron flower, a red label, saturated coral and purple studio fields, hard sunlight. Zafra's references (Erewhon, Ghia, Magnum, Jacquemus) point the same way. **Content follows the launched brand**, held together by the brand book's logo, colour and type rules.

## Who it's for

The style-conscious host in the GCC, 20 to 40, who knows true luxury is quiet and is committed to non-alcoholic hospitality. And the cafés, hotels, luxury retailers and gifting curators who serve them. Full detail: `brand/audience.md`.
