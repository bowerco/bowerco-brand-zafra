# Voice

How Zafra sounds when it writes. Source: Zafra Brand Guidelines v1.2 (November 2025), "Personality & Voice" and "Core Messages".

## Language

English only for our content. (The brand book also covers Arabic pairings; see `brand/visual.md`.)

## Personality

Warmth, grace and quiet assurance. Zafra writes like a thoughtful host: fashion-level taste, sensory and human, never flashy. Calm, composed cadence; confident positivity that feels naturally uplifting.

## Philosophy

- **Poetic clarity.** Lead with the sensory story (light, depth, tone), not technical claims.
- Honour heritage without lingering in nostalgia.
- Confidence is always quiet. **No superlatives.**
- Human words over industrial terms.
- Fewer words, better chosen.

## Do

- Sensory, poised descriptors: "soft floral aroma", "saffron glow", "calm clarity".
- Keep the serving ritual assured: "served neatly, chilled".
- Present Zafra on its own, ideally chilled, over ice if desired.

## Don't

- Reductive visual descriptions ("golden color", "red color").
- Loud superlatives ("high quality", "best drink").
- Any functional claim ("energy boost", "designed for performance") or health claim (see `brand/facts.md`).
- Ever suggest mixing it into cocktails. Zafra is served neat.
- Call it an energy drink, a syrup, a soda, a novelty flavour or a cocktail base.

## Writing rules

Hard rules (claims, serve, punctuation, captions, CTAs): `brand/writing-rules.md`. In short: grace and rhythm, clean measured sentences, minimalist headlines, and every piece still sells.
