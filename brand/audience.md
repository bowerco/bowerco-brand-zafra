# Audience

Source: brand book v1.2 (Nov 2025, written before launch). TODO: confirm against Instagram and TikTok insights (`results/`) and retail data.

## Primary: GCC, 20 to 40
The style-conscious host who knows true luxury is quiet. Stocks their table from premium grocers; every detail (glass, linen, ceramic) is a signature. Wants a refined, rich flavour that fits their commitment to non-alcoholic hospitality. A naturally uplifting moment, any time of day.

## Secondary: hospitality and retail partners
Boutique cafés, hotel F&B leaders, luxury retailers, premium gifting curators. They need a distinctive non-alcoholic offer that photographs beautifully, pours cleanly and reads as a considered choice. Consistency and service clarity matter.

**Trade placement:** menus under **Signature Beverages** or **Apéritifs (Non-Alcoholic)**; retail shelves under **Curated Gourmet Beverages** or **Fine Drinks**.

## Occasions (the modern ritual)
- Personal calm and daytime reflection
- Hosted dinners and intimate gatherings
- **Majlis** hospitality (receiving guests)
- **Ramadan iftar**, **Eid** family visits
- **Premium gifting**
- Weddings and engagements, business meetings
- **Beach season** in the UAE (the cooler months, Emi 2026-11-17)

Always presented on its own, ideally chilled.

## Purchase drivers
Provenance that signals craft (born in Dubai, produced in Italy); genuine saffron (36 mg) with a soft floral profile; clean, measured sweetness; graceful presentation; an effortless ritual.

## Barriers, and how we answer
| They think | We answer |
|---|---|
| "Is it an energy drink or a syrup?" | No: a sparkling saffron drink, served on its own. |
| "Is it too sweet?" | A clean, measured finish, made consistently in Italy. |
| "How do I serve it?" | Chilled, over ice if you like, on its own. |

UAE and Oman (`products/where-to-buy.yaml`). English only.
