# Light tricks: making product photos feel like video

`hero-dappled-light.mp4`: the hero bottle (zaf-ig-030) with warm sunlight through palm leaves drifting slowly across it, plus live grain. The frame never moves; only the light does. The leaf pattern is made from finds/dubai/dubai-04 (placeholder), blurred into soft light.
A shadow version was tried first and dropped: on a dark red background shadows barely show.
