# References

What we take from each reference. **Reference only:** never copy a line, layout or visual.

| Reference | For | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| **Jacquemus** reels | World reel (R2) | One held shot, 5 to 13 s, no cuts. Movement from the scene only. The logo centred over the whole clip. Clean, slightly abstract places; the product often absent. |
| **Erewhon** reels | Product reel (R1) | 7 to 11 s, cuts about every 0.6 s. Alternates ingredient macros, packaging extreme closes and liquid texture. Ends on the full product, upright on a plain seamless, held 2 to 4 s. No text: the label is the branding. One colour family per reel. |
| **Rhode** carousels | Carousel (C1) | A short story per post (topic, context, product, ritual). Mixes stills, macro, real people and short video loops. One text system on every slide: small bold label plus a column of body text. A recurring series name. Product colours used as abstract texture. |
| **Limousine** story sets (Bower) | Story set (S1) | One message, one action. One line per frame that builds into a sentence. Same link sticker in the same spot on every frame. Images build to a payoff. |
